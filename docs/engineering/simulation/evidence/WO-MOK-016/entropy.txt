1. One value per index, derived rather than assumed
---------------------------------------------------

`choose_index` rejects a value below `bound.wrapping_neg() % bound`. Both bounds in play
divide 2^64, so the threshold is zero and nothing is ever rejected for modulo bias:

    bound 128   wrapping_neg() % bound = 0
    bound  64   wrapping_neg() % bound = 0

So a coordinate attempt costs exactly two values, and the draw count below is exact.

Control -- a bound that does not divide 2^64 would reject, which is what makes the check
above load-bearing rather than decorative:

    bound 100   wrapping_neg() % bound = 16
    bound  61   wrapping_neg() % bound = 16
    bound   3   wrapping_neg() % bound = 1

none of the three. That is `REQ-MOK-053`'s *No entropy draw is taken by validation, damage
computation, cost application or death handling* discharged structurally rather than by
sampling, and the same enumeration discharges the identical constraint in `REQ-MOK-052`,
`REQ-MOK-054`, `REQ-MOK-055` and `REQ-MOK-056`.

**The `social` source draws in one branch, and the enumeration says which.** `entropy`
reaches the body of `SocialDecisionSource::decide` exactly once:

    body                     lines 1104 to 1154
    `return` statements      5 -- branches 1 to 5, at lines 1111, 1122, 1129, 1135, 1145
    entropy at line   1153  tolerant_search_choice(observation, entropy)

Every `return` precedes it, so branches 1 to 5 cannot reach the stream by construction and
branch 6 draws exactly once through `tolerant_search_choice`. `post/branches.md` §2 counts
branch 6 at **37,975** of 118,201 decisions across the fifteen traced cells; with this
enumeration that count *is* the source's entire draw consumption, and the other 80,226
decisions are derived. This is the claim `branches.md` defers to this file, and what makes
it a measurement rather than a restatement is the enumeration above: the count is the draw
total only because no other branch has a draw site to reach.

8. What this reader does not establish
--------------------------------------

It does not re-measure the engine. The draw count is reproduced from the placement
arithmetic and checked against the engine's placed cells, which is a strong check on the
reconstruction and no check at all on whether some later code path draws. That claim is
carried by the in-crate assertion named in section 6 and by `shared_stream_draws`.

It does not exhibit the state at a mid-run resolution. The encounter tests construct their
world and resolve immediately, which is why their state is computable here; a resolution
reached by running the world sits at a position no released stream reports, because the
engine has no command that prints the stream. Bounding that would need an engine-side
diagnostic this work order did not add, and it is recorded as a residual rather than
implied to be covered.
