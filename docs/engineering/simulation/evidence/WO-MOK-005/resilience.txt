Test: verification::a_finished_run_stays_inspectable_and_exportable (resilience case)

The wall clock above is measurement only. It never reaches the engine and never reaches a
frame: rule 6 uses it to decide when to draw and rule 1.2 to decide when to advance, and
`verification::no_frame_carries_an_environment_value` asserts no frame carries a clock
value.

## The retained buffer is bounded

The observer retains a window of 100000 records and drops the oldest when full, reporting the
truncation rather than hiding it. Memory is therefore bounded for an unbounded run, and the
engine binary's text stream remains the unbounded record.

records after 10,000 ticks   68948
truncated                    false

       export::tests::the_closing_line_states_the_count_and_the_truncation

## A deliberately delayed run

seed 777, 30 ticks, 5 ms of sleep inserted before every advance so the run falls past
several advance intervals at every speed the observer offers.

wall clock       322.7508ms
records          872 observed, 872 unobserved
streams          IDENTICAL

Falling behind slows a run and never advances two ticks in zero elapsed time, because the
next advance is measured from the last one rather than from a schedule.
