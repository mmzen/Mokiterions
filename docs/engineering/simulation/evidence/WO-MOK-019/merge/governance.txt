### One finding this packet's own correction opens, and it is a real obligation

The measured tree has fifteen warnings where the merge commit has fourteen. The additional one
is:

    ARCH-MOK-002 predates newer declared conforms_to target SPEC-MOK-004 and may require
    reassessment.

It fires at `e8114ad` and not at `1e09f85`, and the cause is act 3 itself: `ARCH-MOK-002` is
dated 2026-08-20, `SPEC-MOK-004` was dated 2026-08-20 at the merge commit, and act 3's
correction moves `SPEC-MOK-004` to 2026-08-21. Same-day became earlier-than.

This is not noise to be dismissed as a date artefact, and it is not reported here as one.
`ARCH-MOK-002` declares `conforms_to SPEC-MOK-004`, and what act 3 changed in `SPEC-MOK-004` is
substantive: rule 9's per-target table, rule 10's `render.rs` private-item figures, and rule
11's three one-invocation totals. An architecture that declares conformance to a specification
whose measured figures just moved is exactly what this rule is for. The obligation the harness
names — reassess `ARCH-MOK-002` against `SPEC-MOK-004` as amended — is owed to its owner, and it
is owed whether or not the warning had appeared.

It is not discharged here, and this packet does not touch `ARCH-MOK-002`. Doing so would be a
second document correction outside the four acts the owner authorized, and the owner's standing
instruction for corrections in this session is that they are drafted for ratification one at a
time rather than applied. It is carried to the owner as a named consequence of act 3 instead.
