Why this is a short file and not a second full packet
-----------------------------------------------------

The first merge's packet measures 120 capture cells, six oracles and 303 MB of stream. None of
it is re-taken here, and the reason is one measurement rather than a judgement:

    git diff efe20e3 -- mokiterions-core          empty
    git diff origin/master -- mokiterions-tui     empty

`mokiterions-core` in this tree is byte-identical to this chain's at `efe20e3`, and
`mokiterions-tui` is byte-identical to `master`'s at `7f4792a`. Pull request #37 changed four
files, all of them the observer's — `src/render.rs`, `src/state.rs`, `tests/state.rs`,
`tests/verification.rs` — and no file of the engine. The record stream is the engine's output.
So every stream figure in `../oracle1/` through `../oracle6/` is a figure at this tree as well,
and re-capturing 120 cells would reproduce the same bytes at a cost of 303 MB to demonstrate a
diff that is already empty.

    Stated as a boundary rather than as a result: the two empty diffs are what license the
    carry-forward, and they are the whole of the licence. Had either been non-empty this file
    would not exist and the captures would have had to be re-taken. The claim is not "the
    engine probably did not change"; it is that no byte of it did, on a comparison that names
    the two trees and prints nothing.

What the observer's byte-identity licenses in the other direction is the figure side of
`SPEC-MOK-004`: rules 9, 10 and 6 count the observer, so their figures on this tree are
`master`'s approved figures and are not this chain's to re-derive. They were re-measured rather
than carried over, and they agree.

Authority
---------

Every command in this file is derived, read-only evidence, and each prints its own boundary.
Validation does not approve. Inspection does not validate by exit status, approve, authorize,
verify, release or remediate. Preflight does not approve artifacts, authorize a diff, verify work,
release software, commit, push, tag, publish or deploy. Nothing here records a decision. The
verification record at the merge commit — `VREC-MOK-019`, not yet written — is where a decision
about this tree is recorded, and it binds a commit.
