===============================================================

This file exists because WO-MOK-025 stop-and-escalate condition 5 requires an escalation
before any schema value is written, and an escalation is worth more with the figures in
it. Nothing here is a change and nothing here proposes one. It is a measurement of what
the tree emits and what the specification says it should emit.

--- What SPEC-MOK-006 says ------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 5.3, at docs/engineering/simulation/specifications/SPEC-MOK-006.md:242

    5.3 `schema` is the stream's schema version, an integer, `2` at this specification. Rule 10 governs it, and the
    2026-08-21 amendment record states what moved it from `1`.

Rule 10.2, at the same file:439

    10.2 `schema` is incremented when a record kind is added or removed, a field is added, removed or renamed, a field's
    type changes, a value's domain in rule 3.2 gains or loses a member, or the ordering rule 9.1 changes. It is not
    incremented by a change that cannot alter any byte any conforming writer produces.

The specification's two conforming header examples, at lines 239 and 584, both read
"schema":2, as does the deliberate violation example at line 686. The engine writes 1.

--- The divergence is known, deliberate, and unratified --------------------------------

The 2026-08-21 amendment row of SPEC-MOK-006 states it itself:

    **The engine does not conform until it is changed**: at `1e09f85` and at `e8114ad` it
    writes `"schema":1` on the header of a stream carrying every field this row adds. The
    change is `RECORD_SCHEMA_VERSION` and one asserted literal in the header unit test,
    and it is **not made here**, because it is a product change and this row is a
    specification correction the owner has not ratified.

That row's approval cell reads **OUTSTANDING** as of 2026-08-21, and it names three
undecided things: the schema increment together with the two-line product change it
obliges, in one commit; the nesting of a targeted verb's target inside proposal; and the
unconditional presence of suffered.

What this capture adds is one fact the row could not state: the divergence still holds at
cc54185, a third commit beyond the two it names, and it holds in all twenty configurations
in both trace modes rather than in the two cells the row measured.

The unconditional presence of suffered is independently visible in this capture as well:
wo-019-comparison.txt section 3 shows 1451 of 3225 records of one cell differing from the
same cell at de33d744 by that field alone.

--- Why WO-MOK-025 runs into this -----------------------------------------------------

WO-MOK-025 adds a fifth decision source named llm. Two rule 3.2 domains gain a member:
config.policy and result.source. Rule 10.2 requires an increment when "a value's domain
in rule 3.2 gains a member", so this work order cannot write a header record without
choosing a schema value, and the value is not derivable while the row is OUTSTANDING:
it is one more than whatever the ratification leaves standing, which is 2 if the owner
ratifies the pending increment and 1 if they do not, giving 3 or 2 respectively.

Guessing it would put a wrong version number into a stream that later becomes
provenance, which is the reason condition 5 exists. No schema value is written anywhere
in this commit.
