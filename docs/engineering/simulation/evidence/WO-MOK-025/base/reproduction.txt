the manifests are what detect a reproduction that failed. This file is that claim exercised
once, on the manifest whose instrument does not exist at the base commit and therefore has
the furthest to go.

--- What this does not establish --------------------------------------------------------

The two capture manifests, base/nosink-manifest.txt and base/sink-manifest.txt, were NOT
round-tripped this way. They come from capture.sh, which needs no patch — it drives the
committed CLI — and reproducing them costs the full eighty runs and 246 MB of scratch
output. They are reproducible by the same argument and were not re-measured a second time,
and that is a difference in how much work was done rather than in how much is claimed.
