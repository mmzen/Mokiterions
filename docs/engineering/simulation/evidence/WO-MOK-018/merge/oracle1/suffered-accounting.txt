Comparisons A, B, D, E, F and G compare **text** streams and pass on every cell. Each of E and F
closes with the same disclosure:

    # 90 right-hand cells additionally carry a record stream, not compared here

This file is that comparison. It exists because oracle 1's own discipline — bytes, no projection,
no tolerance — is what makes a record stream comparison worth having, and because the answer is not
"identical": the merge changes every `action_trace` record the candidate commit wrote. A packet that
compared only the text streams would report six PASSes and leave 3,981,726 changed bytes unexamined.


What changes, and the one reason it changes
-------------------------------------------

Master's combat resolution gives `action_trace` a new field. `SPEC-MOK-006`'s record for that event
carries the subject's state at the moment it acted, and master's rule 26 adds one more fact to that
state: the strikes the subject absorbed inside the window that opened on it. Serialized, on a
subject that absorbed nothing:

    ...,"fear":0,"suffered":[]}}

and on a subject that absorbed one:

    ...,"fear":90,"suffered":[{"attacker":"M05","damage":29}]}}

`,"suffered":[]` is fourteen bytes. That is the whole of the change to the stream's shape, and the
sections below establish that it is the whole of the change to the stream's bytes.


Comparison H: the candidate's record streams against the merge's
----------------------------------------------------------------

`comparison-h-candidate-vs-merge-records.txt`, ninety cells, comparing `sha256(sink)`, sink bytes
and sink lines from `WO-MOK-018/post-sink-manifest.txt` against this session's capture:

    # 90 cells compared: 45 record streams byte-identical, 45 differing
    # 45 of the 45 differing accounted for exactly by the field insertion
    # action_trace records across the capture: 284409
    # total byte difference: 3981726 = 14 x 284409
    # result: PASS

Read that in three parts.

**Forty-five streams are byte-identical.** The `traceoff` half of the capture — 45 of the 90 cells —
writes no `action_trace` record at all, so it has nowhere for the new field to go, and its record
stream at the merge is the candidate's record stream to the byte. Every other record kind is
untouched by master's work: `header`, `run`, `metrics`, `world_initialized`, `agent_initialized`,
`agent_died`, `food_initialized`, `food_consumed`, `food_regenerated`,
`food_regeneration_skipped`, `survival_changed`, `territory_crossed`, `decision_source_selected`
and `simulation_ended` all round-trip identically. Forty-five identical digests is a stronger
statement than any argument about which records the field reaches, because it is the set of cells
where the field reaches nothing.

**Forty-five differ, and every differing byte is accounted for.** Per cell, the byte difference is
exactly fourteen times that cell's `action_trace` count — `+19992 = 14 x 1428`, `+168000 = 14 x
12000`, and so on for all forty-five — and the sum is `3981726 = 14 x 284409`. The instrument fails
the cell, and the comparison, on any other amount.

**Line counts are equal on every one of the ninety cells.** The field is inserted into records that
already exist and adds none. This is the check that distinguishes a field arriving from a record
arriving, and it is the one a byte total cannot make: 3,981,726 extra bytes could equally have been
a few thousand new records. It was not.

The thirty social cells have no counterpart in this comparison and are not silently omitted: the
candidate commit has no fourth policy at all, so there is no candidate-side stream to compare them
to. Comparison G covers them against master and passes on all thirty; what master does not have is
the record stream, and what the candidate does not have is the policy. The merge is the first tree
that has both, which is why the sections below measure the social capture directly rather than
differentially.


The field, on 402,610 records
-----------------------------

`suffered-field.txt`, both captures:

    capture                          mcap-sink    msoc-sink
    cells                                   90           30
    records, all kinds                 961,105      404,779
    action_trace records               284,409      118,201
    ... carrying result.suffered       284,409      118,201     every one, both captures
    ... suffered == []                 284,409      118,066
    ... suffered non-empty                   0          135
    entries across non-empty lists           0          135
    list lengths seen                     none        {1:135}
    entry key sets seen                   none   {(attacker, damage): 135}
    bytes of `,"suffered":[]`        3,981,726    1,652,924
    bytes of non-empty occurrences           0        5,940
    suffered on any other kind            none         none

Four facts here are worth stating as facts rather than leaving in the table.

**The field is present on every `action_trace` record and on no other kind.** 284,409 of 284,409 and
118,201 of 118,201, and `suffered on any other kind: none` in both captures. It is not conditional
on the policy, on combat having occurred, or on the subject having been struck: under the three
policies that cannot attack it is present and empty 284,409 times out of 284,409. That is what makes
the byte accounting above exact, and it is what a specification has to say about the field — a key
that is always present with an empty list, not a key that appears when there is something to report.

**Every non-empty list holds exactly one entry, and every entry has exactly the keys `attacker` and
`damage`.** 135 lists, 135 entries, one key set. Measured, not assumed: the instrument collects the
sorted key tuple of every entry it sees and prints the distinct set, so a third key anywhere in the
30 streams would appear in that column.

    Stated as a limit rather than as a result: one entry per list is what these thirty streams
    produce, not what rule 26 permits. A window that two attackers strike into would carry two, and
    nothing in this capture exercises that. The `SPEC-MOK-006` amendment carried to the owner
    specifies `suffered` as a list for that reason and does not cap it at one; this measurement
    licenses the entry's shape and does not license a maximum length.

**The 135 non-empty lists cost 5,940 bytes and are confined to the fourth policy.** They are the
only bytes in either capture that the empty-form accounting does not cover, and they occur in a
capture with no candidate-side counterpart, which is why comparison H's total is a clean multiple
of fourteen.

**`suffered` is the last key of `result` in all 402,610 occurrences.** Measured separately: every
empty occurrence ends the line as `,"suffered":[]}}` and every non-empty one closes `result`
immediately after the list, with zero occurrences anywhere else in either capture. So the change to
each record is an append at a fixed offset, no other field's serialization moves, and the ordering
`SPEC-MOK-006` fixes for `result`'s keys is extended at the end rather than disturbed.


Twelve event kinds, then fifteen, measured in the stream
--------------------------------------------------------

`record-kinds.txt`. `interface.txt` records `EventType::ALL` going from `[Self; 12]` to `[Self; 15]`
as one changed line in the engine's enumeration. This is the same fact measured in the bytes the
engine emits:

    capture      cells   distinct event kinds   record types
    mcap-sink       90                     12   event, header, metrics, run
    msoc-sink       30                     15   event, header, metrics, run

    attack_resolved       in  0 of 90 cells      in 30 of 30 cells
    threat_resolved       in  0 of 90 cells      in 30 of 30 cells
    surrender_resolved    in  0 of 90 cells      in 30 of 30 cells

Exactly twelve kinds under `baseline`, `reference` and `individual` across five seeds, three
densities and both trace settings; exactly fifteen under `social`; and the three additional kinds
appear in every single social cell rather than in a lucky few. Both directions matter. Twelve under
the older policies is master's combat being confined to the policy that declares it — the fourth
policy is selectable and does not become the default, and the stream proves it rather than the
option parser. Fifteen in all thirty social cells is the new kinds being reachable rather than
merely declared.

The four record types are unchanged at `event`, `header`, `metrics` and `run` in both captures.
Master adds event kinds and no record type; Phase 4a adds the record types and no event kind. That
is the merge's whole shape in one line, and neither parent's contribution collides with the other's.

What this leaves open is stated plainly, because it is the finding this file feeds to the owner.
Phase 4a's `SPEC-MOK-006` was written against twelve event kinds and specifies a record shape for
each. The merge emits fifteen. The engine's own test
`every_event_kind_has_its_exact_record_shape` passes at the merge — 298 of 298 in `gates.txt` —
because it reads `EventType::ALL` and the code covers all fifteen; the specification is what does
not. Three record shapes, five `result.detail` values, a closed `target_died` domain and fourteen
field rows are missing from it, and the amendment drafted for the owner's ratification is derived
from the streams measured here rather than from reading master's source. `README.md` records it as
a finding of this packet.


Every string value in the stream, by field path
-----------------------------------------------

`value-domains.txt`, both captures. `SPEC-MOK-006` rule 3.2 is an enumeration of every string
value the stream may carry and rule 3.3 is the closure argument that makes the stream
escaping-free — "**this is why the stream needs no escaping function, and it is the only reason**".
Master's combat adds string-valued fields, so the closure argument is a claim about the merge and
not only about the branch. This is that claim measured over 1,365,884 records.

**No character outside rule 3.3's union appears in either capture.** The union is `A`–`Z`, `a`–`z`,
`0`–`9` and `_ . - + : ; >`; the characters the two streams actually use are

    -.0123456789:;>ABDFGHKMNQSTVWXZ_abcdefghiklmnoprstuvwxyz          90 cells
    -.0123456789:;>ABDFGHKMNQSTVWXZ_abcdefghijklmnoprstuvwxyz         30 cells

with no quotation mark, no reverse solidus, no space and no code point below U+0020 in either. The
instrument FAILs the capture on any character outside the union and names it; there were none. That
is rule 3.3 holding across the merge, and it holds for the reason the rule gives rather than by
luck: every added value is an identifier, a fixed word, or a colon-joined integer.

**The merge adds five string-valued field paths, and all five are confined to the fourth policy.**
Twenty-three paths under `baseline`, `reference` and `individual`; twenty-eight under `social`; the
difference is exactly

    result.proposal.target        14,206 occurrences   12 values, all M[0-9]{2}
    result.target                  1,552               12 values, all M[0-9]{2}
    result.recipient                 190               12 values, all M[0-9]{2}
    result.target_died               312                2 values, `no` and `yes`
    result.suffered[].attacker       135               12 values, all M[0-9]{2}

and none of the five occurs once in the ninety cells. `target_died`'s domain is measured closed at
two words, which is what lets the amendment state it as a closed domain rather than as a rendering
of a boolean: it is the string form `SPEC-MOK-001` fixes for that field, exactly as `status` is
`accepted` or `rejected` rather than `true` or `false`, so rule 4.5 still admits exactly two
booleans.

**`result.proposal.target` is measured inside the proposal object, not beside it.** The path is
`result.proposal.target` and there is no `result` sibling of `proposal` carrying a target on an
`action_trace` record. The three new field paths that are `result`'s own siblings account for
themselves exactly against `record-kinds.txt`: `result.target` occurs 1,552 times, which is
`attack_resolved`'s 312 plus `threat_resolved`'s 1,240; `result.target_died` occurs 312 times, which
is `attack_resolved`'s alone; and `result.recipient` occurs 190 times, which is
`surrender_resolved`'s. Not one of the three occurs on an `action_trace` record, and the trace's own
target is reached only as `result.proposal.target`. The text line renders the two as
adjacent fields and the record nests one inside the other, so the projection rule 6.4 states
key-for-key has one place where a record field and a text field do not correspond one to one. The
engine's own `every_text_event_line_is_reconstructible_from_its_event_record` covers it from the
other end, over both captures, line for line as bytes.

**`result.proposal.action` carries eleven verbs across the two captures and never more than ten in
one.** Four under the older policies — `eat`, `move`, `sleep`, `wait` — and ten under `social`, which
is the seven targeted verbs plus `eat`, `move` and `sleep`. `wait` is proposed by no social
opportunity and no targeted verb is proposed under the other three, so the union is eleven and the
two halves overlap in three.

**`result.detail` gains three patterns and reaches one word it had not reached before.** Eighteen
digit-generalized shapes under `social` against fifteen under the older policies; the arrivals are
`damage:<u8>`, `increase:<u8>` and `transferred:<u8>`, and the word `out_of_bounds`, which rule 3.2
already enumerates, is reached for the first time in these captures by a targeted move with no valid
axis. `waited` appears in the ninety cells and in no social cell, for the same reason `wait` does not.

    Stated as a limit rather than as a result, because it is the sharpest gap in this measurement:
    **the eight words master's targeted validation can put in `detail` are exercised by neither
    capture.** They are `target_unknown`, `target_dead`, `target_is_actor`, `target_not_perceived`,
    `target_not_in_contact`, `target_not_in_record`, `target_co_located`, and `target_missing`,
    which sits on two arms guarded by `debug_assert!` and is unreachable while the invariant holds.
    The ninety cells carry 284,409 traces and reject none of them; the thirty social cells carry
    118,201 and reject 44, every one of them `out_of_bounds`. So the amendment enumerates those
    eight words from the engine's source and from its unit tests, which do assert every one, and
    not from either stream. Three words rule 3.2 already enumerates — `agent_dead`, `energy_full`
    and `food_unavailable` — are likewise unexercised by both captures and were unexercised before
    the merge; that is a pre-existing property of the enumeration, which is a superset of what a
    capture reaches, and it is safe in that direction under rule 3.3.

`result.proposal.direction` is measured at four values in both captures, `east`, `north`, `south`
and `west`, against the eight rule 3.2 enumerates. A proposal's move is cardinal; the diagonals are
perception's. That, too, is the enumeration being a superset and is not a defect.


Reproducing it
--------------

The captures are this session's, taken at the merge tree by `WO-MOK-018/capture.sh` for the ninety
cells and `merge/capture-social.sh` for the thirty, and they are not retained: 303 MB of record
stream across the 120 cells, 213 MB and 90 MB. The manifests are retained, and the manifests are
what comparison H reads.

    python merge/record-field-accounting.py compare \
        WO-MOK-018/post-sink-manifest.txt  <this session's sink manifest>  <capture dir>
    python merge/record-field-accounting.py field    <capture dir> [<capture dir> ...]
    python merge/record-field-accounting.py kinds    <capture dir> [<capture dir> ...]
    python merge/record-field-accounting.py alphabet <capture dir> [<capture dir> ...]

`compare` needs the capture directory as well as the two manifests, because the expected byte
difference per cell is fourteen times a figure only the stream itself carries. It exits by printing
`PASS` or `FAIL` and names every cell it could not account for; there were none.

The positional check — `suffered` last in `result` on all 402,610 occurrences — was taken as a
line-suffix count over both captures and is reported above rather than retained as a separate file,
because its output is the four numbers quoted.
