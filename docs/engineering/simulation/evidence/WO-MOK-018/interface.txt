Measured on the implementing tree with the temporary oracle removed, and again on the tree at

before figures are read off a tree rather than quoted from the rule they are checking.

Rule 6 counts the observer package only. `SPEC-MOK-002` rule 5 closes the engine's interface
and is not this figure. `mokiterions-tui/src/lib.rs`'s seven `pub mod` lines are the module
declarations themselves and are in neither figure, which is what makes the table below sum to

from the `pub`-line figure, as rule 6 requires.

Counting rule, verbatim from rule 6: one item is one declaration written `pub` outside every
`#[cfg(test)]` block; a public field is part of the item that declares it and is not counted
again as an item; the `pub`-line figure is one line per item plus one per public field.
