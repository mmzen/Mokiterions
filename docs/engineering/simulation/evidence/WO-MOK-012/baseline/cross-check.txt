VER-MOK-012 oracle 1: the pre-change capture against WO-MOK-011's measurements
==============================================================================

Work order   WO-MOK-012 (Phase 3.1, encounters)
Baseline     39662d13abd08e3410648d1c59ad38384f8ad2d2   (COMMIT.txt)
Date         2026-08-20

Two checks the baseline commit makes available for free. Both test the capture rather than the code,
which is the whole of their value: they are taken before any line of WO-MOK-012 is written, and what
they can find is a harness or a machine that does not reproduce a figure another work order recorded.


1. The 90-cell matrix against WO-MOK-011's post-change capture
--------------------------------------------------------------

WO-MOK-011 declared the same 90-cell matrix and took its post-change capture with the same script, at
the candidate tree its README names, `9ddcf83`. Its digests are in the repository at
evidence/WO-MOK-011/post/post-manifest.txt.

From that tree to this baseline, the engine's source changes by eighteen lines and none of them is
executable:

    $ git diff --stat 9ddcf83..HEAD -- mokiterions-core/src Cargo.toml Cargo.lock
     mokiterions-core/src/lib.rs        |  2 +-
     mokiterions-core/src/simulation.rs | 16 ++++++++--------
     2 files changed, 9 insertions(+), 9 deletions(-)

    $ (the same diff, with comment and blank lines removed)
    (no non-comment engine line changed)

The eighteen are the 007-to-010 renumbering inside doc comments and one test-module banner. The
observer moved for real over the same range -- 356 lines of mokiterions-tui/src/render.rs, the colour
bands of WO-MOK-007 -- but the observer writes nothing to the engine's standard output, which is what
capture.sh captures. So every one of the 90 cells is predicted to be byte-identical across the two work
orders, and it is:

    $ awk '/^seed/ {print $1, $2, $4, $5, $6}' evidence/WO-MOK-011/post/post-manifest.txt | sort
    $ awk '/^seed/ {print $1, $2, $3, $4, $5}' evidence/WO-MOK-012/baseline/pre-manifest.txt | sort
    $ diff of the two

    cells in each manifest:                                     90
    identical on raw SHA-256, bytes, lines and exit:            90
    differing:                                                   0

WO-MOK-011's manifest carries a projected digest in column 3, which this one does not, so the columns
are selected by name above rather than the files diffed whole. That projection is WO-MOK-011's and
belongs to the field it added; VER-MOK-012 oracle 1 compares baseline unprojected, and manifest.py says
why at length.

Three cells are held whole on both sides. Their digests agree, and this is why retain.py does not copy
them into this packet a second time:

2. The workspace test census against WO-MOK-011's post-merge census
-------------------------------------------------------------------

baseline/test-census.txt reports 212 test names, all passing. The comparable figure is not
WO-MOK-011's post/test-census.txt, which reports 205: that census was taken at the candidate, before
the merge that brought WO-MOK-007's band tests in. The comparable figure is its merge/test-census.txt,
taken at the merged tree, and it reconciles exactly:

    $ diff <(names from WO-MOK-011/merge/test-census.txt) <(names from WO-MOK-012/baseline/test-census.txt)
    (no output)

    names in each census:                                      212
    identical, target-qualified, name for name:                212

The seven names the 205-census lacks are the band tests -- five under `unittests :: render::tests::`
and two in `tests/render.rs` -- and their absence there and presence here is the merge, not a drift.

analysis/census.py is WO-MOK-011's script copied unchanged, deliberately: a reconciliation name by name
between two work orders is only sound if both censuses were taken by the same reader of the same log
format. "Unchanged" is meant literally and is checkable --

    $ cmp WO-MOK-011/analysis/census.py WO-MOK-012/analysis/census.py
    (no output)
    $ sha256sum both
    7d35545477539fc56ebcd89efbfc790bb4860409b8156cbf603d8c316bc61567  (both)

-- which is why its own docstring still says VER-MOK-011 and its usage line still names that packet's
path. Correcting those two strings would have cost the byte equality that makes this paragraph
verifiable, and the string is not what the census depends on.


3. What these checks establish, and what they do not
----------------------------------------------------

They establish that the same matrix and the same test gate, taken on a different day by a different work
order, produce the same 110 MB to the byte and the same 212 names: the harness is deterministic across
invocations and the figures recorded here are not an artifact of this machine's state today.

They establish nothing about the change WO-MOK-012 makes, which has not been written yet.

They are not a substitute for capture-state.txt. Reproducing another work order's numbers says nothing
about whether *this* worktree was clean when these numbers were taken; that claim is measured
separately, from git archive.

And they do not discharge anything owed elsewhere. WO-MOK-011's merge/README.md records its oracle 1 as
not re-taken after the merge, and the agreement above is the first 90-cell measurement at the merged
tree -- but it is taken against WO-MOK-011's *post* digests rather than the projected comparison against
`524a675` that its oracle states, so whether that oracle is satisfied is that work order's verification
record's business and not this file's.

RESULT: PASS -- 90 of 90 cells and 212 of 212 test names agree with WO-MOK-011's measurements, as an
engine whose only change is comments predicts.
