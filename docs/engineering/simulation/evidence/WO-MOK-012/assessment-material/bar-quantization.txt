bar    values          span

So at the reference roster the proportional fill distinguishes 0..=49 from 50..=99
from 100, and nothing finer. Two attributes 49 apart draw the same bar. The level is
carried by the three-digit numeric value, which is why SPEC-MOK-003 rule 2.5 still
holds without colour — but the fill is not doing the work the rule's wording implies
