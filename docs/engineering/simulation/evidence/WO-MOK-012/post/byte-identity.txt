-------------------------

2. The `baseline` clause: satisfied
-----------------------------------

    "The 30 `baseline` cells must be byte-identical, unprojected, with identical exit codes."

30 of 30, unprojected, exit 0 on both sides. This is the load-bearing claim of INT-MOK-009 -- that a
capability about Mokiterions acting on each other changes what a Mokiterion *chooses* and nothing about
what the world *does* -- and it is measured rather than argued. Rule 4's candidate list, rule 9's eat
effect, the food table, rules 14 to 16 and the shared entropy stream's consumption are all reachable
from a `baseline` run, so a change to any of them would appear here; the seven new verbs, the two new
trace fields and the four new resolutions are reachable from none of it.

It also carries a second fact worth stating plainly, because it is easy to lose: the `social` source
draws no entropy for any targeted decision. Had it drawn one, the shared stream would still be
untouched in a `baseline` run -- `baseline` never runs the social source -- so this comparison does not
establish that. Oracle 2 does, and it is a separate owed item.


3. The `reference` and `individual` clause: NOT satisfied
--------------------------------------------------------

    "The 60 `reference` and `individual` cells are expected to differ, and each difference must be
     attributed to REQ-MOK-051's corrected waste condition by a stated characterization -- first
     diverging tick, first diverging record, and the eat-or-seek decision that changed -- not merely
     noted."

Sixty of sixty are identical. There is no divergence to characterize, and the absence is not a
convenience: it is REQ-MOK-051 measured as unimplemented.

REQ-MOK-051 requires that "no calorie class hold more than half of any territory's standing resources"
and states where the correction may be made -- "in the waste condition of the decision sources", rule
5's `satiety + restoration <= 100` and rule 19's tolerant form. Neither waste condition was touched by
98a85ea. So the two sources propose exactly what they proposed at the baseline, their streams are
unchanged, and the requirement's obligation is unmet: measurement at this candidate fails 14 of the 15
seed-and-territory cells the requirement bounds.

That state is the product owner's decision of 2026-08-20, recorded rather than discovered here: the
composition correction is deferred to a second amendment, so that REQ-MOK-014's and REQ-MOK-034's
floors are re-measured once against a world holding both properties rather than twice against
intermediate worlds that never ship -- which is the reasoning REQ-MOK-051's own rationale gives for
pairing the two changes in the first place.

So this section is a recorded failure of an approved requirement, not an oracle awaiting evidence. It
is listed as such in escalation.md, and WO-MOK-012 cannot reach `implemented` while it stands.


4. What this comparison does and does not settle
------------------------------------------------

Settles      that the world's behavior is unchanged under the three pre-existing sources, on the whole
             declared matrix, without projection.
Settles      that REQ-MOK-051 is unimplemented at this candidate, by the absence of the divergence its
             own text predicts.

Does not     say anything about the `social` source, which does not appear in these 90 cells.
             post/social-manifest.txt records those thirty separately and provisionally.
Does not     establish the no-entropy constraint (oracle 2) or the resolution arithmetic (oracle 3).
Does not     survive an amendment to REQ-MOK-051, whose whole point is to move 60 of these 90 cells.
             When that amendment lands, this file is superseded and the characterization oracle 1 asks
             for becomes writable for the first time.

RESULT: MIXED -- the `baseline` clause passes on 30 of 30 cells; the `reference` and `individual`
clause is unsatisfied because REQ-MOK-051 is unimplemented, which is a recorded deferral and an open
obligation, not a measurement gap.
