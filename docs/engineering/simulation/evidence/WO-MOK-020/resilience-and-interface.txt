Resilience, the viability floor, and the public interface
========================================================

Three of VER-MOK-004's checks are measurements against figures the contract fixed in
2026-08-17 for WO-MOK-004. Two of those figures no longer describe this repository,
because master has moved since: phase 3 and the resource-composition ceiling both
landed. The measurements are therefore taken base-against-candidate, which is the
comparison that answers whether THIS change perturbs anything, and the divergence
from the contract's stale numbers is reported rather than hidden.

STALE FIGURE. The contract's bullet says the run completes "at the same termination
ticks as recorded before". WO-MOK-004's retained resilience-10k.txt records
reference/0.75 as extinction at tick 9154 with survivors 0; it is now tick_limit at
10000 with survivors 2. That is master moving between 2026-08-17 and 2026-08-22, not
this change moving it: base and candidate agree exactly. The clause cannot be read
against those numbers any more, and doing so would report a false regression.

2. The 1,000-tick population floor at the declared density
---------------------------------------------------------

From nonperturbation/summaries-after.txt, the reference source at density 0.75:

  seed      0    1   42  123  777
  survivors 8    9   10    9    9      candidate
  survivors 8    9   10    9    9      base f7b1c45 (identical manifest)

Every seed is at or above the per-seed survivor floor of eight that REQ-MOK-014 and
VER-MOK-002 state, and every cell is byte-identical between base and candidate, which
is the stronger of the two claims.

STALE FIGURE. The contract fixes these as "8, 11, 8, 9, 11 on seeds 0, 1, 42, 123,
777" and asks for identity with them rather than merely clearing the floor. Measured
at f7b1c45 they are 8, 9, 10, 9, 9. Same cause as above; the floor of eight still
holds on every seed.

3. The public interface did not grow
-----------------------------------

  $ git diff f7b1c45 -- mokiterions-core/src mokiterions-tui/src | grep -E '^[+-].*pub'

returns exactly one pair of lines, and they are the same item: the observer's

  -pub const USAGE: &str = "Usage: mokiterions-tui [--seed <u64>] ... ";
  +pub const USAGE: &str = concat!(

One escaped single-line literal became a concat! of one literal per line. The name, the
visibility and the type are unchanged, and both constants are still
`pub const USAGE: &str` — cli.rs:21 and options.rs:24. No public item was added,
removed, or changed in signature, so SPEC-MOK-002 rules 5 and 6 and SPEC-MOK-004's
equivalent hold, and the observer's new test reaches mokiterions::cli::USAGE, which was
already public and is read by the engine's own tests.

`cargo doc --no-deps --lib -p Mokiterions` lists 30 public items. It also emits one
warning, `public documentation for 'suffered' links to private item 'SufferedAttack'`
at mokiterions-core/src/simulation.rs:1542. That is inherited from master and not from
this work order: `git diff f7b1c45 -- mokiterions-core/src/simulation.rs` is empty. It
is recorded here because a reader running cargo doc will see it, and rustdoc is not one
of the repository's gate commands, so nothing else would mention it.
