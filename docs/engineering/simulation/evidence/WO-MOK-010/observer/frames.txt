VER-MOK-010 — oracle 4: rendered buffers, cell by cell
======================================================

Work order   WO-MOK-010 (Phase 2.5, naming)
Baseline     524a6758d74b5240079959e9827ea40a7af22a30
Date         2026-08-19

Every claim below is asserted against an in-memory character buffer produced by ratatui's
`TestBackend`. No screenshot, recording, terminal or pseudo-terminal is involved, which is what
REQ-MOK-029 and SPEC-MOK-004 require of an observer rendering claim.


Method
------

`observer/frame-dump.rs` is a throwaway harness that reaches the observer only through its public
interface — `options::parse`, `Observer::new`, `Observer::advance`, `Observer::handle_key`,
`render::draw`, `layout::resolve`, `spatial::Viewport` — and prints, for each renderable viewport:

  * the pane rectangles rule 5 resolves;
  * the roster pane's rows as text, border included, each labelled with its absolute `y`;
  * for every roster row carrying an identifier, the absolute cell of that identifier and the six
    columns immediately before it, printed verbatim, beside the name the engine reported for that
    identifier — read from the records, never from a literal;
  * every canvas cell holding a letter or a digit, with its absolute coordinates, which is every
    Mokiterion glyph (food is `○◎●` and the territory rule is `─`);
  * where each living subject maps, by the observer's own `Viewport::cell_of`, so the glyph scan and
    the mapping can be joined by the reader;
  * the inspector pane's rows as text.

It then prints the detail-zoom canvas with a living selection, and the inspector for a **dead**
selection.

The harness is written to compile against the pre-change revision as well: it parses `name:` out of
a record only if the record has one, and prints `<no name on this revision>` otherwise. It was run
twice — once in this tree, and once in a scratch tree extracted with
`git archive 524a675 | tar -x` and built offline — so the two sides of the bar-row comparison come
from one harness rather than from two.

    cargo test --test zz_frame_dump -- --nocapture      (both trees)

Retained here:

    observer/frame-dump.rs     the harness source, since the file is deleted from the tree
    observer/frames-post.txt   the candidate capture, 916 lines
    observer/frames-pre.txt    the baseline capture, 916 lines
    observer/bar-rows.txt      the 96 bar rows the harness printed, at all nine viewports
    observer/bar-rows.diff     empty: the two sides are identical

The declared viewports are VER-MOK-005's, reused: `160x48`, `160x44`, `160x40`, `140x44`, `140x43`,
`120x48`, `120x30`, `100x30`, `34x22`. `33x21` is below the floor and renders nothing, so it is not
in the harness's set. The configuration is seed 42, `--start-paused`, one `advance`, the reference
policy at density 0.75.

The bar row: 96 rows, at nine viewports, identical
--------------------------------------------------

    grep ' h .*  s .*  e .*  f ' frames-pre.txt  | sort > bars-pre
    grep ' h .*  s .*  e .*  f ' frames-post.txt | sort > bars-post
    diff bars-pre bars-post
    (no output)                     96 rows on each side

This is REQ-MOK-041's arithmetic claim measured rather than argued: `BAR_ROW_OVERHEAD` is 35 and
`bar_width(45)` is 2 before and after, because the name is on line one and line two never sees it.
At the 47-column roster the bars are two cells wide (`h ██ 100`), which SPEC-MOK-003 rule 4 as
amended accepts; the amendment did not change that width, `WO-MOK-007`'s fourth gauge did, and this
capture confirms naming did not change it again.

What the whole diff contains
----------------------------

    diff frames-pre.txt frames-post.txt      570 differing lines

Every one of them is one of four things: a roster or inspector row whose head gained six columns or
a name; a printed cell position that moved from `x=1` to `x=7`; a canvas cell whose glyph changed
from a base-13 digit to an initial; or the harness's own report of what the records said. No pane
rectangle changes, no bar row changes, no pane appears or disappears at any viewport, and no cell
outside a roster head, an inspector heading and the twelve glyph cells differs at all.
