Scenario 5: a stream that no longer exists is re-derived from its coordinates alone.

The 400 rows' file has sha256 a22e3e6b678e07b33c9cb13f249d06e353d34e53666c153e5794e18797874cdc.
Its first 16 hex digits seed a Mersenne twister, which chooses index 133 of 400.
That row's coordinates are source=reference density=0.25 seed=13, ticks=1000.
