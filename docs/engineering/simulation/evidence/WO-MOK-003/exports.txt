# Exports — WO-MOK-003

`SPEC-MOK-002` rules 9.4 to 9.6: an export carries authoritative records only, in
authoritative order, in the `SPEC-MOK-001` line format, and it ignores any active filter.
It is the observer's only retainable artifact and the only observer output admissible as
evidence.

An export is the engine's records followed by one closing statement, `# retained=N
truncated=B`. The engine binary's stream is the same records followed by its `summary` line.
Comparing the two therefore means comparing every line but the last of each.

## What is retained here

`exports/events-seed<N>-ticks20.log`, one per declared seed, complete with its closing
statement. Twenty ticks rather than the default hundred keeps each file reviewable: a
default run is about 1,400 records and 134 KB, so five of them would add two thirds of a
megabyte of generated log to the repository. The byte comparison below is performed at both
tick limits, and the default-configuration figures are retained as counts and digests.

## Reproducibility

seed 0    ticks 20    74855 bytes    623 records   two runs, one interacted with andfiltered: BYTE-IDENTICAL
seed 1    ticks 20    74909 bytes    622 records   two runs, one interacted with andfiltered: BYTE-IDENTICAL
seed 42   ticks 20    74811 bytes    623 records   two runs, one interacted with andfiltered: BYTE-IDENTICAL
seed 123  ticks 20    75001 bytes    621 records   two runs, one interacted with andfiltered: BYTE-IDENTICAL
seed 777  ticks 20    74717 bytes    622 records   two runs, one interacted with andfiltered: BYTE-IDENTICAL

A filter changes what is presented and never what is exported, which is why the second
run above is filtered before its export is taken.
