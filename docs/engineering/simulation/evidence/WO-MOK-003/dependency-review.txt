### Why the lockfile is larger than the compiled surface, and why 'serde' appears in it

`Cargo.lock` records the maximal resolution: every optional dependency of every resolved package,
whether or not the feature enabling it is on. `serde` is listed there as an optional dependency of
`ratatui`, `ratatui-core`, `ratatui-widgets` and `wezterm-input-types`. The observer enables none of
those features, so `serde` is absent from the resolved graph above and is never compiled.

$ grep -n '^name = "serde' Cargo.lock
1126:name = "serde"
1136:name = "serde_core"
1145:name = "serde_derive"

packages in Cargo.lock whose dependency list mentions serde:
  ratatui, ratatui-core, ratatui-widgets, wezterm-input-types  (all optional, all off)

The figure that matters for the containment claim is the resolved graph, not the lockfile:
`cargo tree -p mokiterions-core` is a single line, so nothing in the lockfile reaches the engine.
