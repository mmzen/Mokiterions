Pre-change   pre/COMMIT.txt      Candidate  post/COMMIT.txt

None of the three floors is inherited by this candidate. Each was ratified on the world this
work order corrects, so each is measured again here on the shipped build. A floor the corrected
world misses is stop condition 5 and is the owner's to answer; this reader states the verdict
per seed and does not encode it in an exit code.

**The direction is a net regression on survival, and it is stated that way because that is what
was measured: 9 of the 15 obligated runs leave fewer Mokiterions living after the correction and
2 leave more.** No floor is missed and none of the movement is large, but a reader who
expected "eats more, so survives better" would have it backwards, and the reason is worth
stating rather than leaving to inference:

    resources consumed across the whole 120-cell matrix, pre-change   26,136
    the same figure at the candidate                                  26,924
    standing resources at tick 1,000 over the 15 obligated runs, pre   1,680
    the same figure at the candidate                                   1,595

The correction admits eats a Mokiterion would previously have declined, so the world is grazed
harder: 788 more resources are consumed across the matrix and 85 fewer are standing at
the horizon in the runs the requirement binds. Depleting the standing stock is exactly how the
composition ceiling is met -- `post/composition.txt` measures the high class falling from 81.6%
to 54.1% at its worst -- and a thinner larder is the price. The per-run figures above do not
line up one-for-one with the survivor movements, so this is offered as the mechanism and not as
a per-seed explanation; attributing an individual death would need the divergence analysis.

This is the trade `WO-MOK-017`'s approval measurement disclosed before the condition was
ratified, in the owner's sight: over the 50 unbound seeds `0`-`49`, cells above the ceiling fell
from 140 of 150 to 7 of 150 while cells below their source's floor rose from 6 of 150 to 17 of
150. The declared set behaves the same way in miniature and no declared seed misses a floor.

One consequence points the other way at a shorter horizon and is recorded in
`post/updated-tests.md`: at 200 ticks the correction removes starvation-driven health decline
entirely under `reference` and `individual` on every declared seed. Both facts are consistent --
feeding sooner helps an individual early, and grazing the world harder costs the population
later -- and the pair is why the 1,000-tick horizon is the one the floors are stated at.

---------------------------------------------------------------
    seed  commit      strikes  lethal strikes  combat deaths  attrition deaths  bound
       0  pre-change        8               1              1                 2   met

       1  pre-change       14               2              2                 0   met

     123  candidate        22               3              3                 1   met
     777  pre-change        8               1              1                 0   met
     777  candidate         6               1              1                 2   met

The lethal-strike column and the combat-death column are produced by two different rules --
counting `target_died:yes` across the cell, and testing the record adjacent to each
`agent_died` -- and they must agree cell by cell. They do, in all 120 cells at both commits,
which is what licenses reading either as the lethality figure.

`REQ-MOK-058`'s bound is an existence claim per seed, "at least one death attributable to
combat", and whether it should become a rate is a deferral the requirement carries and this
work order does not touch. The per-seed counts are printed anyway, because a bound met by one
death on a seed is a different fact from one met by three, and the deferral is decided on that.

6. Lethality is measurable without tracing, checked rather than assumed
----------------------------------------------------------------------
    social triples where the traced and untraced cells agree on every
    strike, lethal strike, combat death, attrition death and survivor   30 of 30

`attack_resolved`, `threat_resolved` and `surrender_resolved` are emitted whether or not
actions are traced, so the lethality bound is measurable in all 120 cells. That is a claim
about the released instrumentation rather than about this change, and it is measured here
because the whole of section 5 rests on it.

No floor is stated at either density and none is read here. `REQ-MOK-014`'s *Notes* say why in
