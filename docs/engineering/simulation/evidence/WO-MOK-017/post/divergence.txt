1. The two conditions, from the two commits' own source
------------------------------------------------------
Attribution is arithmetic, so the arithmetic is transcribed from each commit's
`mokiterions-core/src/simulation.rs` rather than from the specification. The specification is
the thing the pre-change binary had drifted from, so quoting it for both sides would beg the
question this file exists to answer.

    pre-change  rule 5   S + R <= 100
                rule 19  S + R <= 100  ||  S + R - 100 <= T*R/100
    candidate   both     S + R <= 100  ||  S + R - 100 <= max(R*R/100, T*R/100)
                         with T = 0 for rule 5, which is `fits_within(food, 0)`

The drift was one term omitted twice: rule 5 had no allowance at all, and rule 19 had only the
trait half. That is also why rule 19's `T = 0` identity with rule 5 held on the drifted pair --
at `T = 0` the trait term is zero and both collapse to `S + R <= 100`. The identity was a
coincidence of the defect, and the amended clause 1 together with the shared `fits_within` is
what makes it structural.

    class    R   R*R/100    highest satiety the old rule 5 ate at    the candidate's, at T = 0
    low      15        2                                       85                           87
    medium   30        9                                       70                           79
    high     50       25                                       50                           75

`reference` ignores the trait, so a `reference` cell's printed `waste_tolerance` is not its
allowance and is not read as one here: its old allowance is zero and its new one is `R*R/100`.

2. The model checked against both captures before it is used
-----------------------------------------------------------
Every consumption prints the satiety it happened at, so each condition can be tested against
the capture of the commit that implemented it. A consumption outside its own commit's allowance
would mean this reader's model of that commit is wrong, and every attribution built on it with
it.

    consumptions in the 90 obligated cells, pre-change       26,102
    the same at the candidate                                26,890
    pre-change consumptions above a satiety of 100            3,482
    the same at the candidate                                12,128
    pre-change consumptions outside the OLD allowance             0
    candidate consumptions outside the NEW allowance              0
    candidate consumptions the OLD condition cannot emit      8,626

    **Neither capture holds a single consumption outside its own commit's allowance.** Both
    conditions are reproduced exactly by the two captures' own records, so the model this
    attribution rests on is measured rather than assumed.

    The check has teeth. An earlier draft of this reader modelled the old condition as plainly
    `S + R <= 100` for every source, and the 3,482 pre-change consumptions above a
    satiety of 100 contradicted it at once: they are the trait-aware sources eating inside the
    old `T*R/100` term, which the drifted rule 19 did have. The correction to this reader came
    from the capture, not from re-reading the source a second time.

3. The three attribution routes
-------------------------------
Two sources differing only in their waste condition can behave differently only where the two
conditions disagree: some class with `S + R > 100`, admitted by the new allowance and declined
by the old. Call that the disagreement band. Each cell's first divergence takes one route.

    R1  direct         the first diverging record carries the acting Mokiterion's pre-action
                       satiety, and that (S, T, source) is in the band. The changed decision is
                       visible in the record itself.
    R2  stream-shared  the acting Mokiterion is not in the band, but an in-band one acted
                       earlier in the same tick with an unchanged record. A wider candidate set
                       consumes a different number of draws even when it reaches the same
                       proposal, so the next Mokiterion to draw wanders somewhere else.
    R3  sibling        an untraced cell, explained by the traced cell of its own triple --
                       same seed, source and density, diverging no later, by R1 or R2. An
                       untraced stream prints no proposal, so the changed decision is invisible
                       in it and its first visible difference is a downstream effect.

    cells attributed by R1                               57 of 90
    cells attributed by R2                                9 of 90
    cells attributed by R3                               24 of 90
    cells attributed by no route                          0 of 90
    obligated cells that did not diverge at all           0 of 90

    **Every obligated divergence takes one of the three routes.**

4. The independent necessary condition: nothing may diverge before the band is entered
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The three routes classify a divergence. This is the check that can refute one. Every
Mokiterion starts at a satiety of 100 and loses one per tick, so no band is reachable at tick 0:
the first one a falling satiety enters is the low class at 87 -- excess 2 against an earned
allowance of 2 -- and 87 is thirteen decrements below the start. A divergence arising before any
Mokiterion is in a band could not be this correction's, whatever route it appeared to take, so
the earliest band entry is measured per cell from the pre-change stream and compared with it.

    starting satieties, over all 90 obligated cells         [100]
    earliest first divergence, over all diverged cells      tick 14
    latest                                                  tick 28
    earliest band entry measured in a pre-change stream     tick 14
    cells whose divergence precedes their own band entry    0

    **No cell diverges before the tick at which its band is first entered.** The bound is
    not slack: the earliest divergence and the earliest band entry fall on the same tick,
    so the correction takes effect at the first observation where it can. That is the
    strongest form this check can return, and it is why no divergence in any of the
    90 obligated runs sits earlier than tick 14 -- a Mokiterion at a
    satiety of 88 or above declines every class under both conditions alike.

    cells whose waste_tolerance assignment moved            0
    Rule 20 fixes the trait at initialization from the seed, and it is identical at both
    commits in every cell. So these divergences are the condition reading the same trait
    differently, not a different trait being drawn.

diverging record. `T` is its `waste_tolerance` as printed at initialization -- shown for every
source, including `reference`, where the trait is in the record but the source does not read
it. Each `R1` row carries the excess above 100 and both allowances, so its arithmetic is
checkable without rerunning this reader. On an `R2` or `R3` row the `S` and `T` shown are the
ones the routes had to *reject* -- that Mokiterion is not the one whose answer changed, which
is precisely why those cells take a route other than R1 -- and the witness column names the one
that carries the explanation. Where the two sides of the line are different record kinds, both
are printed separated by a slash.

7. The decision that changed, by kind
-------------------------------------
Both sides of the first differing line are named, and they are not always the same kind of
record: where one commit emits a resolution the other does not, the line indices part and an

sides would read as though a `food_consumed` had changed its proposal.

A verb of `-` means the record carries no proposal, which is every record kind but
`action_trace`. Those rows are the untraced cells, plus the traced cells whose diverging line is
a resolution one commit emits and the other does not. The changed decision behind them is named
in the route and witness columns of section 5, not here.

    traced cells, where a proposal is printed and can be the first difference   45
    untraced cells, where the earliest visible difference is an effect          45

The two halves of a triple part at the same decision but report it differently, and that is
instrumentation rather than behavior. In a traced cell `action_trace` prints the proposal, so
the first differing line names the verb that changed. In an untraced cell the proposal is not
printed at all, so the first differing line is whatever the changed decision eventually
produced -- a `food_consumed` one commit emits and the other does not, a `survival_changed`
for a Mokiterion that is somewhere else, or a `food_regenerated` whose class and position have
moved because the shared stream is at a different offset. That is why route R3 exists, and why
an untraced cell is explained by its traced sibling rather than by its own first record.

Rule 5 applies its condition in two places and both appear above: case 1, eating a co-located
resource, and case 3, approaching the nearest resource it would not decline. A changed `move`
is the second of those -- the Mokiterion walks toward a resource it now accepts instead of
wandering or heading elsewhere -- and it is the more common first divergence because seeking
happens at a distance and so happens sooner than the eat it leads to.

9. What this does not claim
---------------------------
It does not claim every *subsequent* difference in a diverged stream is individually
attributable. Once two runs part they are different worlds: different Mokiterions are alive at
different positions with different satieties, and every later record differs for that reason
rather than for this change's. Stop condition 3 is written about the divergence, singular per
cell, and the first diverging record is where a divergence is attributable at all.

It does not re-derive the proposal. Whether the corrected condition admits the *right*
resources is a specification question, answered against constructed state in the test suite --
`the_corrected_non_waste_condition_admits_the_specified_boundaries` fixes all three classes at
both boundaries in both of rule 5's cases, and
`a_trait_difference_alone_decides_whether_a_clipped_resource_is_eaten` fixes the trait's
effect. A second implementation of the specification inside an evidence script would agree
with whichever of the two was written second.

Route R2 concedes something worth stating plainly: a divergence can reach a Mokiterion the
condition never applied to, through the draw count of one it did apply to. That is not a defect
and it is not new -- `WO-MOK-016` recorded the shared stream as the mechanism by which any
behavioral change propagates -- but it does mean the *set* of Mokiterions whose behavior moved
is larger than the set whose answer the condition changed. `post/entropy.txt` is where the draw
accounting itself is recorded, and `post/survivors.txt` is where the population consequence is.
