with the byte comparison of the 30 baseline cells and the characterized divergence of the 60 others",
and adds that "a discrepancy is never resolved by recapturing it". pre/capture-state.txt is the same

This packet is the pre-change side alone... the comparison oracle 1 exists for happens later, at the

The matrix is the same 120 cells, taken with the same capture.sh, so the two captures are comparable
cell for cell. 30 of those cells are the byte-identity obligation and 90 are the divergence.

3. The two manifests against each other: which cells moved, and no others
------------------------------------------------------------------------

pre-manifest.txt and post-manifest.txt hold the same 120 cell names. Compared row by row on all four
columns:

    cells in both                                              120
    rows identical at both commits                              30
    rows differing                                              90
    identical rows that are baseline cells                      30 of 30
    differing rows that are baseline cells                       0 of 90

**The partition is exactly the source.** Every baseline cell is unchanged and every non-baseline cell
moved, at every density and with tracing either way. That is REQ-MOK-060's load-bearing constraint and
the divergence obligation in one table, and it is stated here as a partition rather than as two counts
because the partition is the claim: a single baseline cell in the differing set would breach
INT-MOK-010, and a single reference, individual or social cell in the identical set would mean the
correction did not reach a source it was written for.

The two statements this table does not make are made elsewhere and are not restated here.
post/byte-identity.txt compares the 30 baseline cells byte for byte rather than by digest, with their
exit codes, because a digest match is a claim about a hash function as well as about the bytes.
post/divergence.txt attributes each of the 90 divergences to a route and checks that none precedes the
tick at which the corrected condition can first apply.

Exit codes: both manifests report the set ['0'] over all 120 cells. Every run at both commits reached
its stop condition and none was a diagnostic.


4. The prediction pre/capture-state.txt wrote down, measured
-----------------------------------------------------------

pre/capture-state.txt §4 recorded the pre-change eat census and then stated, before the change was
written, what the corrected condition would have to do to it. That prediction is quoted and measured
here, from post/eaten.txt, over all 30 cells of each source:

  Under reference, the prediction was "after the change the same three columns must read 87, 79 and 75,
  and the wasted total must be non-zero and bounded by R * R / 100 per eat":

    highest pre-eat satiety     low       medium      high      cells wasting any     satiety wasted
    pre-change                   85           70        50            0 of 30                      0
    candidate                    87           79        75           30 of 30                 43,800

  All three bounds are attained exactly, as they were before the change, so the condition is inclusive
  at every boundary and reaches every one of them somewhere in the matrix. These are the three numbers
  SPEC-MOK-001's amendment of 2026-08-21 states, read off 120 captured streams rather than off the
  test suite. The per-eat bound is post/divergence.txt §2, which measures 0 consumptions outside their
  own commit's allowance at either commit.

  Under the two trait-aware sources the prediction was narrower: "the effective allowance becomes
  max(R * R / 100, T * R / 100), so the low and medium bounds do not move at all and only high class
  rises, from 70 to 75":

    highest pre-eat satiety     low       medium      high      cells wasting any     satiety wasted
    individual, pre-change       90           82        69           30 of 30                 10,062
    individual, candidate        90           82        75           30 of 30                 46,008
    social, pre-change           90           82        70           30 of 30                  9,508
    social, candidate            90           82        75           30 of 30                 43,458

  Low and medium do not move by a single point on either source, which is the prediction exactly: a
  tolerance of 40 already admitted more than R * R / 100 for those two classes, so max() returns the
  tolerance term and nothing changes. High class rises to 75 on both, from 70 under social and from 69
  under individual. The pre-change figure quoted in the prediction was social's, and 70 is the bound a
  tolerance of 40 admits on both sources: individual's 30 cells span 56 to 69 and simply do not contain
  the eat that attains it, which is a fact about the matrix and not a second bound.

  Under baseline the prediction was that there is nothing here for the change to relax:

    highest pre-eat satiety     low       medium      high      cells wasting any     satiety wasted
    pre-change                   92           77        85            8 of 30                    214
    candidate                    92           77        85            8 of 30                    214

  Every figure identical, which it must be if 30 of 30 cells are byte-identical, and is recorded
  separately because it is read from a different field of the stream than the digest is.

The waste totals are the correction's whole effect stated in one number per source: zero to 43,800
satiety wasted under reference, and roughly four and a half times the previous total under each
trait-aware source. post/survivors.txt §4 is where that turns into 788 more resources consumed and 85
fewer standing at the horizon, and post/composition.txt §6 is where it turns into the ceiling being
met.


5. What is not established here
-------------------------------

- Nothing here is a verification verdict. VER-MOK-016 is the contract and its record is a separate,
  commit-bound act reserved to the assurance owner.
- This file establishes provenance and nothing about correctness. That the retained bytes are the
  commit's own output says nothing about whether the commit is right; the ceiling is
  post/composition.txt, the floors are post/survivors.txt, the attribution is post/divergence.txt and
  the suite is post/updated-tests.md.
- It is not a post-merge record. The candidate is a branch commit and this packet binds it. Per this
  project's convention a verification record binds a branch commit and never master's merge of it, and
  nothing here is written about a merge that has not happened.
- Both scratch captures -- the discarded working-tree one and the reproduction these bytes come from --
  were written outside the repository, at about 156 MB each, and neither is retained. That is why this
  file records the outcome and the commands rather than the streams. analysis/retain.py states what is
  kept instead, cell by cell, and section 2 above states the four commands that rebuild the whole
  capture from the commit it names.

RESULT: PASS -- every byte retained under post/ is the output of 26ae6ba, produced by building and
running that commit's own archived tree; the 120 cells agree with the discarded working-tree capture on
four columns each; the two manifests partition exactly by source, 30 baseline rows identical and 90
others moved; and the three-column prediction pre/capture-state.txt made before the change was written
is met on all four sources.
