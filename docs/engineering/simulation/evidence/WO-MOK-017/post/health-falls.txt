What the table establishes, in the order the argument needs it:

  1. **The guard genuinely fired.** At the candidate, no declared seed under `reference` or
     `individual` produces any fall at all among the Mokiterions alive at tick 200. The test
     could not have been left as it was: it would fail with "this scenario is unexercised",
     which is the guard doing its job rather than a defect.
  2. **`REQ-MOK-060` is why.** Health falls only once satiety reaches zero, so the corrected
     waste condition -- which feeds Mokiterions sooner -- removes the decline at this horizon.
     Seed 42 under `reference` fell 35 points before the
     correction and 0 after it.
  3. **`social` is not a weaker scenario, and it is not a longer one either.** There the
     decline is combat damage rather than starvation: seed 42 falls 78 points
     pre-change and 78 at the candidate. Selecting it decouples the test from the
     nutrition model that has now broken it twice, which is why it was preferred to raising
     the horizon until starvation reappears.
  4. **The guard and the assertions are untouched.** The threshold stays at thirty and both
     properties -- a strictly lower fill at the end, and fill monotone in health across every
     sample -- are asserted exactly as before. `VER-MOK-013` states the scenario as "200
     ticks at a declared seed at the reference viewport" and names no decision source, so the
     source is a parameter the scenario leaves open.

What this does not claim: that `social` will exercise the gauge forever. It is coupled to the
combat model instead of the nutrition model, which is a different dependency and not none. A
scenario that constructs a declining subject directly, rather than searching a live run for
one, is the durable answer, and it needs a `VER-MOK-013` amendment this work order has no
authority to make. Raised in `manual-assessment.md` for the technical owner.

RESULT: PASS -- the guard fails under `reference` and `individual` at the candidate on
every declared seed, and `social` seed 42 clears it at both commits, so the scenario
change is a parameter selection made necessary by the correction rather than a retreat
from an assertion.
