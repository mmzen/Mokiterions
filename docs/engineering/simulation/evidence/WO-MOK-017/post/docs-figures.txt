captures; `outside` from a run the document itself prints the command for; `cited` from a

5. Fear across creature-turns, both commits
--------------------------------------------
    population                              turns      at 100   above 0   +5 steps

    reference and individual, pre-change    111,604      39%      57%        219
    all four deciders, candidate            160,921      34%      52%        540

    per-run share above 0, candidate, ten runs   44% to 64%

One creature-turn is one `survival_changed` record and the fear read is the value it ends
at, so the population is exactly the turns lived and nothing is weighted. The population
shrinks with the correction -- 111,604 turns become 105,445 -- because fewer Mokiterions
reach turn 1,000, which is the same fact the survivor rows of section 2 report from the
other side. That is why the document dates the percentage rather than stating it flatly.

The `+5` step is the clamp at the top of the range: a Mokiterion at 95 gains the usual 10
and stops at 100. It is measured rather than derived because it is the one step size
section 8's table does not predict.

7. What the trait still changes: meals the reference rule would have refused
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    trait-aware runs at the default density, one row per declared seed

    seed    meals   refused by the reference rule of its own commit   creatures
    0         412   3                                              1
    1         366   1                                              1
    42        405   2                                              2
    123       363   3                                              2
    777       388   0                                              0
                    pre-change: 73, 86, 54, 97, 75                 9, 9, 9, 10, 9

Each commit is judged by its own reference rule, which is the only comparison that means
anything: before the correction the reference rule admitted no spill at all, so a meal
counted here is one spilling any; after it, one spilling more than the resource's own
allowance of 2, 9 or 25. The trait went from deciding 54 to 97 meals a run to deciding at
most 3, because on a High resource the allowance of 25 already exceeds anything a
tolerance of 40 can add.

That is a narrowing of the trait, not of the divergence: `post/divergence.txt` accounts
for every one of the 90 obligated cells, including seed 777, whose two runs still part
even though not one of its meals is a meal the reference rule would refuse. A tolerance
also decides what is worth walking to, and a path that differs moves who is standing
where long after it has stopped moving who eats what.

The satiety column is the trace record's own field, which is the value at the moment the
Mokiterion decided -- except on turn 20, where the meal has already been applied by the
time the trace prints, and the document prints that row as `81 -> 96` for exactly that
reason. The last column is the corrected condition's arithmetic on each of those turns,
and it is the whole of the walk: the spill sits above the allowance of 2 for thirteen turns
and reaches it on turn 14, which is the turn the Mokiterion sets off.

This is why the example is in the document twice over. It is a real run, and the correction
is visible in it without a second world to compare against: nothing walks toward a resource
it would waste more than the allowance on, and the turn the arithmetic changes is the turn
the behavior does.
